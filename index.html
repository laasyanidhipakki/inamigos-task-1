Who we are

A small team with a big heart for change.

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit founded on 23 September 2020 by Govind Shukla. We work from Chhattisgarh and across India.

Our focus is practical support — education for children, food and clothing for families, skills for women, care for stray animals, and greener communities.
